# Screenshots for the CurseForge page

Six screenshots, in the order they appear in `docs/curseforge-description.md`. Each is marked there as `[Screenshot N: ...]`.

## Before you take any of them

- Keep character names, guild names, chat and other players out of the frame. Crop to the window you show.
- Move or hide the chat frame and close other windows before each shot, then crop tightly; for the tooltip shots, a quiet spot with no other players nearby helps.
- Turn off name plates and floating names for the shots taken in the world, and keep your own name out of the auction house window's title area.
- Use a resolution and UI scale where the text reads clearly once cropped. Save as PNG.

## 1. Tooltip, Full style

- Setup: settings at their defaults (Tooltip style Full, Other items with this look: Only when not collected, Lowest auction price on). Scan a slot in the Appearances tab first, so a price is known. Then hover, in your bags or on an auction row, an item of that slot whose appearance you have not collected and that has other items with the same look.
- Visible: the game's item lines, then "Not collected" with its source on the right, the "Lowest price" line with its age, and "Other items using this appearance:" with its list.
- Crop: the tooltip only.
- Caption: Every item tooltip says whether its look is collected, where it comes from, and what else gives it.

## 2. Tooltip, Compact style

- Setup: Tooltip style Compact. Hover a not-collected item with other items, then (for a second crop, optional) a collected one.
- Visible: "Not collected +N items" (or "Collected" with its check) on the right of the name line.
- Crop: the tooltip only.
- Caption: Compact style puts the status on the item's name line.

## 3. The Appearances tab

- Setup: the auction house open on the Appearances tab, a slot with many for-sale looks (Chest or Feet works well), Show: Not collected, Sort: Price: low to high, after a scan. Hover one tile so its tooltip shows the lowest price.
- Visible: the slot row, the tile grid with price strips, the hovered tile's tooltip, the scan status at the bottom.
- Crop: the auction house window, with the hovered tooltip.
- Caption: Every look of a slot you have not collected, cheapest first.

## 4. A tile's items for sale

- Setup: on the same page, click a tile with a price, wait for the check, click it again so its list opens.
- Visible: the open list beside its tile, the item rows with prices and their Buy buttons.
- Crop: the auction house window, or the list and its tile with some grid around them.
- Caption: Click a look to see each item for sale and buy it in one step.

## 5. The Filter menu

- Setup: click the Filter button and open the Source or Class submenu.
- Visible: Show, For sale only, Source, Class, Sort, the open submenu.
- Crop: the upper half of the window with the menu.
- Caption: Filter by collected, for sale, source and class; sort by price.

## 6. The settings page

- Setup: type `/tc` (away from the auction house, as the Options window closes it). Defaults, or Full style.
- Visible: the preview with its two sample tooltips, the "Item tooltip" settings and the "Appearances tab" setting.
- Crop: the Options window's right pane, or the whole Options window if the AddOns list shows no other personal addons you would rather not show.
- Caption: Choose the style, colors and what the tooltip lists, with a live preview.
